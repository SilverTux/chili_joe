APPLICANT PRIVACY

Who is responsible for your information?

Controller: Tranadition HTH 68 Kft., 1196 Budapest, Zrínyi utca 93.

What information is collected?

The information you submit in the application form: your name, email address, optional phone number, availability, experience message, and optional CV.

Why is it used?

To review and manage your application and contact you about recruitment.

Who can access it?

Access is limited to authorized hiring staff at Tranadition HTH 68 Kft. Netlify Forms processes submissions on our behalf as a data processor and service provider; its authorized personnel and subprocessors may process them as needed to provide and secure the service. Netlify states that submissions are stored in its secure user database and can be viewed through the site’s Forms dashboard, API, or CSV export. Netlify may process data in the United States and other countries; its Privacy Statement and Data Processing Agreement describe international-transfer safeguards, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses, as applicable. Current subprocessors are listed in Netlify’s Trust Center. Netlify recommends additional protection, such as its Very Good Security integration, for form uploads containing personal information; confirm this setup before accepting CV uploads.

How long is it kept?

Applications are retained for 30 days from receipt, after which they are deleted. Netlify notes that uploaded-file links may remain accessible for up to 24 hours after a form is deleted because of caching.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of your application, you may request access to your personal data, its correction or erasure, restriction of processing, object to processing, or request data portability. Contact us at 6chicks.info@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, including Hungary’s National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH).
