APPLICANT PRIVACY

DRAFT — Before accepting applications, Chili Joe’s must complete and approve this notice with its legal controller details, privacy contact, lawful basis, retention period, and the relevant Netlify processing and transfer information.

Who is responsible for your information?

Controller legal name, registered address, and privacy contact: to be supplied by Chili Joe’s before launch.

What information is collected?

The information you submit in the application form: your name, email address, optional phone number, role of interest, availability, experience message, and optional CV.

Why is it used?

To review and manage your application and contact you about recruitment for the role you selected.

Who can access it?

Access should be limited to authorized hiring staff. The form is processed using Netlify Forms; the controller must confirm and disclose the applicable provider, contract, and any international-transfer details before launch.

How long is it kept?

Retention period: to be set and approved by Chili Joe’s before launch. Applications should be deleted when no longer needed for the stated recruitment purpose.

Your choices and rights

You may ask about or exercise your data-protection rights by contacting the controller. Add the approved privacy contact and applicable complaint information before publishing this notice.
